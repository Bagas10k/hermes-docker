## 2. The 6 World Archetypes (Rotated Dynamically)
1. **Swiss Editorial:** Monolithic condensed display typography (112px–127px, line-height 0.93), 95% achromatic canvas (`#F8F8F8`, `#111111`), 5% singular punch accent (`#FA5D29`), hairline borders (`#EDEDED`).
2. **Neo-Brutalism:** Solid black outlines (2px–3px `#000`), hard unblurred offset shadows (`4px 4px 0 #000`), tactile press translation (`scale(0.975)`), vibrant color blocking.
3. **Liquid Gooey & Jelly Morph:** Two-Layer Silhouette architecture (Layer 0 SVG/WebGPU fluid backdrop, Layer 10 razor-sharp text WCAG AAA >= 7:1), GSAP spring physics `back.out(1.8)`.
4. **Console-First / Cyber Tactile:** Dense telemetry HUD, monospaced data fonts (`JetBrains Mono`), CRT scanlines, obsidian backgrounds (`#0C0D0E`), phosphor emerald/amber indicators.
5. **Spatial Tactile & Physical Glass:** Multi-stop progressive backdrop blur, physical light edge highlights, layered micro-depth.
6. **Asymmetric Bento Grid:** Functional organic cell spans, in-place card expansion, zero rigid modal popups.

## 3. Motion & Anti-Slop Standards
- Solid 60 FPS in-place morphing (GSAP FLIP).
- Strictly banned: generic purple-blue AI gradients, floating decorative glow orbs, meaningless kicker badges, muddy blurred drop-shadows.
