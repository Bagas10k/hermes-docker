# Upnormal World Archetypes & The Law of Zero Rigid Boundaries

## Mandat Utama Mas Bagas: "Upnormal Itu Sebenarnya Tidak Ada Patokan!"
Desain Upnormal bukanlah formula matematika statis atau daftar 7 tema yang mengunci kreativitas. Upnormal adalah **sikap eksplorasi terbuka (Open-Ended Creative Horizon)**:
- **Tidak ada patokan tunggal yang kaku.**
- Agen **wajib secara aktif mencari referensi desain web terbaru di internet** setiap kali hendak membangun antarmuka.
- Dunia web terus berevolusi setiap detik: tren warna, kurva animasi, tata letak asimetris, dan interaksi taktil baru bermunculan setiap hari di Awwwards, Godly, CodePen, Dribbble, dan GitHub.
- Arketipe di bawah ini hanyalah batu loncatan awal (*trampolines of inspiration*), bukan sangkar batasan. Agen bebas menggabungkan, membalikkan, atau melahirkan gaya visual baru yang tak pernah ada sebelumnya, asalkan hasilnya **"WAH", berjiwa seni tinggi, dan bebas dari klise AI Slop**!

---

When the user gives no strict design theme, NEVER default to a cookie-cutter generic AI template. Boldly explore the internet and rotate or invent beyond these archetypes:

## 1. Swiss Editorial (Gallery Wall & Asymmetric Grid)
- **Visual Thesis:** Minimalist-curated modernism inspired by Awwwards Site of the Day and Zurich editorial print.
- **Color Grammar:** 95% Achromatic — Ink (`#111111` or `#222222`), Paper (`#FFFFFF`), Warm Pale Gray Canvas (`#F8F8F8` / `#EDEDED`). Single accent punctuation: Ember (`#FA5D29`) or Klein Blue (`#002FA7`).
- **Typography:** Display title spanning full viewport width (`Inter Tight` or `Syne` at 112px–127px, weight 300–600, line-height 0.93, tracking -0.02em). Hierarchy is scale, not font mixing.
- **Surfaces:** Flat, rectangular, hairline borders (`1px solid #EDEDED`), zero blurry drop-shadows.

## 2. Neo-Brutalism (Raw, Honest & Punchy)
- **Visual Thesis:** Rejecting delicate gloss in favor of high-energy tactile physicality.
- **Color Grammar:** Stark contrast — Pure Yellow (`#FFE600`), Vibrant Mint (`#00F5A0`), or Hot Coral (`#FF4D4D`) paired with pure `#000000` text and `#FFFFFF` cards.
- **Borders & Shadows:** Thick high-contrast borders (`2px - 3px solid #000000`). Hard offset shadows with zero blur: `box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px #000000`.
- **Interactions:** On active click, element translates into its shadow: `transform: translate(2px, 2px); box-shadow: 2px 2px 0px #000;`.

## 3. Warm Earthy Editorial ERP (The Cadra Paradigm)
- **Visual Thesis:** Merging enterprise complexity with luxury consumer editorial calm. Erases the boundary between heavy factory software and high-fashion lifestyle design.
- **Color Grammar:** Warm Alabaster canvas (`#F4F1EA`), Slate Charcoal (`#1E2022`), Terracotta / Burnt Ochre (`#C85A32`), Muted Sage Green (`#4A6B53`), and Powder Periwinkle (`#D8E2EC`).
- **Layout:** Floating pill dock navbar, squircle cards (radius 24px–32px), asymmetrical 65/35 hero split, and 4-column Bento metric deck with alternating tonal fills.
- **Microcopy:** Natural conversational executive summaries ("Good afternoon, Noor. 5 operations on the floor, 2 shipments on the road...") replacing cold data tables.
- **Interactive Timeline:** A single-axis horizontal schedule strip with color-coded multi-state task nodes and a live dynamic time scrubber pin.

## 4. Liquid Gooey & Jelly Morph (Living Fluids)
- **Visual Thesis:** Elastic, organic surfaces that fuse and separate like mercury droplets.
- **Two-Layer Silhouette Architecture:** Layer 0 contains SVG filter blobs; Layer 10 holds razor-sharp text (WCAG AAA 7:1).
- **Physics:** GSAP spring transitions using `back.out(1.8)`.

## 5. Console-First / Cyber Tactile (Terminal Precision)
- **Visual Thesis:** High-density command decks and developer-grade control telemetry.
- **Color Grammar:** Deep Obsidian (`#0C0D0E`), Charcoal Surface (`#141517`), Hairline Border (`#26272B`), Phosphor Emerald (`#10B981`) or Amber (`#F59E0B`).
- **Typography:** Monospaced data headers (`JetBrains Mono`), uppercase micro-labels with `letter-spacing: 0.08em`.

## 6. Spatial Tactile & Physical Glass (Frosted Depth)
- **Visual Thesis:** Tactile multi-plane depth without muddy blurs. Progressive multi-stop backdrop blur, paired with subtle top-highlight border.
- **Interactions:** Tactile spring compression on `:active` (`scale(0.975)`).

## 7. Asymmetric Bento Grid (Organic Densities)
- **Visual Thesis:** Functional asymmetry where cell sizes reflect real content hierarchy. Cells morph in-place on hover/focus rather than popping open in separate dialogs.
