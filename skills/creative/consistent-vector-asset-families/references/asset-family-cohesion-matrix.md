# Asset Family Cohesion Matrix & Benchmark Checklist

To ensure a set of diverse vector subjects forms a unified "family", evaluate each candidate asset against the following 4 objective benchmarks.

---

## 1. The Four Pillars of Family Cohesion

| Pillar | Criterion | Target Threshold | Failure Symptom |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pillar 1: Palette Identity** | $100\%$ shared root gradient stops and shadow tints. | $\Delta E_{00} < 2.0$ across core stops | Asset looks like it belongs in a different theme |
| **Pillar 2: Light Vector** | Unified key light angle from top-left. | Fixed at $-45^\circ \pm 5^\circ$ | Lighting appears chaotic when assets sit side-by-side |
| **Pillar 3: Material Ratio** | Balanced proportion of Metal vs Glass across subjects. | $50\% \pm 15\%$ Metal, $50\% \pm 15\%$ Glass | One subject looks like plastic while others look metallic |
| **Pillar 4: Bounding Weight** | Visual visual mass and center-of-gravity normalization. | Aspect ratio within $0.8 - 1.25$ bounding ratio | One asset dominates or feels dwarfed on a grid |

---

## 2. Multi-Subject Testing Roster (The Quiver Benchmark)

When building or testing a new asset family style, generate at least 5 subjects across distinct categories:

1. **Precision Mechanical:** Vintage Camera or Wristwatch. (Tests fine circular gearing and sharp edges).
2. **Architectural / Functional:** Desk Lamp or Modern Chair. (Tests tubular joints and cantilever balance).
3. **Organic / Curved:** Seashell or Botanical Leaf. (Tests flowing non-geometric Bézier curves).
4. **Vessel / Liquid:** Teapot or Cocktail Glass. (Tests refractive transparency and meniscus).
5. **Dynamic / Vehicle:** Classic Sports Car or Airplane. (Tests aerodynamic curves and ground shadows).

If all 5 subjects look harmonious when displayed side-by-side on a clean white grid, the style signature is proven robust.
