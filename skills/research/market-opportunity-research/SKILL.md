# Market Opportunity Research

Use for a market-entry or company strategy, not merely a competitor news digest. For Bagas, write in concise Indonesian; distinguish an executable service business from an unvalidated universal platform. Record attribution to Bagas when documenting his architecture, without treating outside market claims as his invention.

## Procedure

1. **Frame the decision before searching.** State buyer, geography, workflow to automate, outcome metric, and what decision the research must change. If the user asks about a *local* market but has not named a locality, research national/regional signals and label the local conclusion **belum teruji**; ask for the first kabupaten/kota as the next decision, one question at a time.
2. **Map evidence lanes.** Fetch dated primary statistics (BPS, BI, ministries), official competitor capability/pricing pages, existing local alternatives (including open source and incumbents), and public user reports. Load `agent-reach` for internet and platform routing, run its doctor for multi-backend social channels, and follow its topical references. Use `grounded-citations` for a source ledger while retrieving, inline citations in the report, and mechanical citation verification. A search result is discovery, not a read page.
3. **Separate voices.** Quote or paraphrase user complaints only from a readable first-person post, review, issue, interview, or observed workflow; vendor posts about customers being overwhelmed are *vendor positioning*, not customer testimony. If authenticated social channels, comments, or scraping are inaccessible, use other readable channels and report the coverage gap; never call snippets a representative social sample.
4. **Bound the market.** Separate all enterprises, digitally transacting businesses, reachable prospects, and buyers willing to pay. Do not convert a national UMKM count, QRIS merchants, or a manager survey into TAM or conversion rate. Distinguish IMK (manufacturing subset) from all UMKM. Quote sample/population and publication date with every statistic.
5. **Compare alternatives at job level.** Capture actual public prices with billing period, seats, included messages, setup fees, third-party channel/model charges, and date; mark missing terms unknown. Compare the customer's current manual process, deterministic forms/workflows, agency service, and incumbent software before recommending custom AI. For villages, inspect existing official systems and sensitive-data/approval obligations before proposing a new record system.
6. **Forecast as scenarios, not spurious numbers.** Give baseline/upside/downside mechanisms, observable triggers, disconfirming evidence, and a reversible first bet. Do not claim replacing an entire office role when only bounded tasks have been tested; retain a named human owner for payments, official documents, external communications, and sensitive data.
7. **Design the validation gate.** Propose interviews in one locality about *the last actual failed transaction*, baseline time/error per workflow, then paid pilots. Compare like-for-like median and p95 completion time, correction/exception rate, third-party cost and support hours. Use a kill/pivot criterion based on paid demand and net benefit; label all thresholds and proposed prices as hypotheses until measured.
8. **Deliver a decision brief.** Lead with recommendation and confidence, then evidence, competitor positioning, target segment, short pilot sequence, risks, and one next decision. Put the detailed source-backed research in an artifact when scope is broad; never state field validation or social scraping occurred if only desk research did.

## Pitfalls

- Verify competitor marketing claims as claims *by that vendor*, not measured performance; otherwise you turn sales copy into evidence.
- Do not conflate a closed bug report with a current widespread product failure; its value is a failure mode and a question to test.
- Preserve geographic uncertainty: national digital-readiness statistics cannot rank a specific village's purchasing power or connectivity.
- Count buyer-paid outcomes, not dashboard screens, chat messages, or unverified hours saved; setup/support can erase apparent automation savings.
- Keep quotations and statistics tied to retrieved URLs via the citation ledger; a manually assembled source list is easy to misnumber.
