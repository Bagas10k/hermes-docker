# Privacy research without republishing
Scope incident count, data classes, exposure mechanism, and mitigation before collecting. Use official disclosures, advisories, published studies, or consented participants. Record source URL, date, denominator, and uncertainty. Never copy credentials or private chat text into a report. Distinguish data entered into AI from data disclosed to outsiders. Only report a percentage when its denominator is known. Synthetic fixtures are not breach evidence.
