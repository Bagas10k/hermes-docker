# Offer Eligibility Research

Use for one-off checks of paid products temporarily offered free, student benefits, carrier bundles, referral credits, and trials. A standing price/offer alert is a separate monitoring task.

## Procedure

1. Pin the country of lawful redemption separately from countries of later use, audience (student, new user, carrier customer), product tier, and whether the user wants *zero-spend* or merely a waived subscription fee. Verify both redemption and cross-border use in primary terms; a global announcement or foreign-only offer does not establish either. Do not conflate a free-tier product with a paid tier on promotion.
2. Search broadly for candidates, then open the **provider's current offer page and terms**. If access comes through a partner, check both provider and partner terms. Prefer current eligibility/checkout documentation over old announcements and snippets. Never treat a search-result excerpt as confirmation that a claim still works.
3. For each candidate, record exact tier, benefit duration/limits, country or institution eligibility, new/existing-account rules, verification method, payment-card requirement, upfront purchase or carrier-plan cost, auto-renewal price, claim deadline, and cancellation consequences. If a term cannot be confirmed, label it unknown rather than assuming. Distinguish availability in a country from confirmed eligibility for the *specific promotion*.
4. Categorize results: **claimable under stated conditions**, **conditional/unverified at account checkout**, **bundled with a paid purchase**, and **excluded/expired**. Never call a paid bundle 'free without spending'. Never claim an offer has been redeemed without observing the account's entitlement.
5. Answer with a short prioritized list and direct official links beside each claim. Put the material caveat (card, renewal, paid prerequisite, region) on the same line as the benefit. If the user's eligibility is unknown, suggest checking the official claim flow rather than requesting credentials or payment details.

## Verification

Before sending, confirm that each recommended offer has a current primary source, the country and exact tier match, and an expired or region-restricted offer has not slipped into the positive list. For changing promotions, timestamp the check; avoid promising continued availability.

## Pitfall

Do not infer that a globally advertised student plan grants the same premium tier everywhere: providers may offer Pro in one country and Plus elsewhere, so compare the regional terms before naming the benefit.
