# Company operating manuals for AI-assisted solo businesses

Use this workflow when producing a manual that must onboard both people and AI, rather than merely assess an opportunity.

## 1. Lock facts, assumptions and delivery

- Extract first buyer, first paid workflow, existing product capability, delivery surface, founder capacity, financial authority and selected document format.
- Separate founder-provided facts, retrieved facts, recommendations and model assumptions. Record unresolved decisions without pretending they were approved.
- If the user requests a planning step only, stop at that step. Once a full manual is authorized, continue the agreed scope without repeatedly requesting approval for already authorized drafting.
- Use one modular master and a separate concise AI onboarding brief. Put templates inside the master appendices unless independently requested as working files.

## 2. Research then draft in chapter order

For each chapter, identify its load-bearing questions, retrieve appropriate primary sources, register evidence, then write the chapter before advancing. Parallel helpers may collect independent research; their summaries are not verified source facts.

For important external claims, attach the source URL/ID, access date and scoped confidence. Distinguish enacted legal text from agency guidance, vendor definitions, founder self-reports and proposals. Use the grounded-citations workflow for the ledger and relevant literal evidence.

Start every chapter with the required short summary. End each chapter with decisions and validation items. Check their actual placement, not just their global count; appendices added later must not push the closers into the middle of the final chapter.

## 3. Keep the economics and operations consistent

- Define package names, seats, active skill counts, quotas, overages and third-party charges once, then reference that canonical table.
- Show model inputs and formulas before results. Simulate scenarios with reproducible arithmetic, distinguish revenue from cash, and include founder draws and reserves without calling them statutory expenses/rates.
- Count human review, support, onboarding and sales time. Flag growth scenarios that exceed capacity instead of inventing staff not budgeted in the model.
- If the go-to-market changes from a mixed portfolio to a first focused segment, either recalculate with justified inputs or explicitly label the old mix a sensitivity exercise, not the current forecast.
- Make refund, top-up expiry, cancellation and unused-balance handling executable rules rather than ambiguous prose. Obtain appropriate professional review before turning them into contract terms.

## 4. Write executable policies and acquisition work

Each SOP needs a trigger, numbered actions, AI/human owner, tools, output, checklist and escalation. A subprocedure may explicitly inherit its parent's fields. Distinguish internal targets from signed SLAs; do not add unmeasured uptime guarantees for completeness.

Marketing should identify buyer role, recurring pain, discovery channel, consent boundary, demo proof, CTA, funnel events and paid-pilot acceptance. A calendar is an experiment schedule, not proof that impressions produce customers. Prioritize legitimate company-level research, relevant business contact routes, permission and opt-out; do not automate harvesting or unsolicited mass messages.

Keep legal templates usable with explicit fields and annexes, but label professional review, unknown legal status and implementation gaps. A privacy/security policy is not proof that the application enforces it.

## 5. Apply QC with evidence, not obedience

Ask independent reviewers for bounded, concrete findings against the final candidate and actual model/source files. Verify each finding before changing the manual. Reject suggestions that misread rounding, confuse first shortfall with lowest balance, replace buffers with assumed tax rates, delay incident clocks until complete confirmation, or invent contract commitments.

Check final chapter order, summaries and end sections, calendar coverage, table/model reconciliation, canonical terms, current buyer/offer consistency and citations. Test counts in their relevant sections so similarly named roadmap rows do not produce false failures.

## 6. Produce the requested artifact and stop

Apply confirmed decisions before native-format conversion. For Word use the docx skill, preserving native headings, editable tables and sources. Provide the master and brief directly, with optional evidence files kept separate. State the distinction between document checks, professional review, market validation and product runtime tests; do not reopen unrelated implementation work at delivery.
